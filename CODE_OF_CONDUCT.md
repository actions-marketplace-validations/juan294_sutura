# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act in ways that support an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Standards

Positive behavior includes respectful language, constructive feedback, empathy, and a focus on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults, trolling, personal or political attacks, publishing private information without permission, and other conduct that is inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to `juan294@gmail.com`. Reports will be reviewed promptly, fairly, and confidentially.
